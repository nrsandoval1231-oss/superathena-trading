# Hypothesis

These modules record controlled experiments and negative knowledge. They are not a statement that a later paper arm inherited their results.

`aat_v1` asked whether a memory arm improved decision quality under a frozen claim contract. The consumed holdout cannot be used again. `aat_v2` compares exogenous and actor treatments under a separate protocol. Neither package is allowed to send a broker order.

Failure conditions for any future use: reading an `INVALID_EVIDENCE` artifact as if it were fresh evidence, renaming the packages, or promoting either experiment without a new prospective test.
