# Hypothesis

Names that gap 4–12% on the first regular session after a known report can be paper-traded in the gap's direction, with a stop at half the gap and a same-session exit, when Jev accepts the setup and the shared account gate has room.

The screener's historical band is a watchlist rule. This book does not rerun that study and does not claim the band is an edge. A headline heuristic is not confirmation from an issuer release or a filing.

Failure conditions: a gap outside the band, an unresolved prior exit, a second reservation of the same symbol, a cap breach, or any new order that would remain open overnight.
