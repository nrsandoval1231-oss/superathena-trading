Athena Trading is a dual-strategy adaptive trading system: **Earnings Adaptive** and **Athena Predictive Intraday**, sharing one Athena Core, one decision workflow, one risk layer, one execution lifecycle, and one continuous learning loop.

**Two Arms. One Mind. Continuous Wisdom.**

## Canonical requirements

1. **Two product strategy arms.** Earnings Adaptive is event-driven and thesis-based. Predictive Intraday is signal-driven and prediction-based. Both use the shared components below. ORB-M can supply an intraday signal, baseline, or comparison; it does not replace either arm.
2. **Hybrid model ownership.** Nick approved a generative LLM + Jev + deterministic-code architecture on 13 October 2026 and explicitly selected **Astra 6** as the LLM. Astra 6 interprets source-linked, timestamped events/news/guidance and produces structured theses, scenarios and proposed plans with expiry and invalidation. Jev judges bounded choices using both the underlying evidence and the proposal, including an insufficient-evidence outcome. Code owns calculations, validated forecast outputs, freshness, sizing, shared portfolio risk, execution and APDE transition enforcement. The current pilot is still Jev-only; this amendment does not implement or deploy the hybrid.
3. **Decision vocabulary.** LONG, SHORT, NO_TRADE, HOLD, ADD, REDUCE, PROTECT, TRAIL, and EXIT are the conceptual actions. A strategy may use a bounded subset. Every action becomes a structured proposal; none directly calls a broker.
4. **Point-in-time world state.** A decision reads market data, signals/features, predictions, positions, and account/risk state frozen as of its decision time. Record event time and availability time. Missing or stale information is visible, never invented.
5. **Athena context.** Retrieve relevant validated experiences, similar situations, lessons/rules, failure modes, and regime context with provenance and version identity. Future outcomes and unvalidated lessons cannot enter a decision packet.
6. **Earnings intelligence.** Use earnings calendars/estimates, guidance/sentiment, options skew and implied-volatility context, historical reactions, catalysts, and risk mapping. Each source must meet the point-in-time data contract.
7. **Earnings thesis/scenario engine.** Form beat/meet/miss scenarios, reaction distributions, expected moves/ranges, confidence and edge estimates, and a trade plan. Code supplies quantitative distributions/ranges only from validated calculation or forecasting components. Astra 6 may interpret them and propose qualitative scenarios, but its unsupported numbers remain hypotheses, not forecast facts. Calibration, horizon, sample support and uncertainty must accompany a quantitative forecast; unavailable quantities stay unknown.
8. **Earnings execution/positioning.** Express pre-event, post-event, or intraday entry logic, position sizing, instrument structure, and correlation/risk requirements through the shared proposal and execution path. Stocks, options, and multi-leg trades are target capabilities; supported instruments/windows are explicit implementation policy.
9. **Intraday market intelligence.** Use multi-timeframe data, technicals/order flow/VWAP, volume/volatility regimes, news/social/macro evidence, correlations, and sector context where trustworthy data exists.
10. **Intraday prediction engine.** Produce a short-horizon forecast, probability of a defined target, expected MFE/MAE/duration, path forecast, confidence, and invalidation; update on material fresh evidence under bounded call/latency budgets. These numbers come from validated forecasting/calculation code, not uncalibrated model confidence. The diagram's +1% is an example target, not a universal objective or guarantee.
11. **Intraday execution/positioning.** Propose entry signals/confluence, size, leverage/instrument choice, stops, and management through the same shared risk and execution workflow as earnings.
12. **Shared APDE.** Both arms manage positions through EXPLORE → PROTECT → HARVEST → DEFEND → EXIT. Respectively: allow the thesis room to develop; confirm/reduce risk; let winners run; tighten/protect profit; exit when the thesis breaks or edge disappears. Astra 6 can propose an updated plan and Jev can judge a permitted management choice, but code enforces state transitions from arrived evidence and reconciled exposure. Stops, kill switches, emergency exits and mandated session exits must not wait for either model. State transitions remain subject to risk limits.
13. **Deterministic risk is final authority.** Enforce position/exposure/leverage limits, correlation limits, daily/weekly/monthly limits, maximum drawdown, stop requirements, kill switches, circuit breakers, and applicable instrument/session constraints. Neither Astra 6 nor Jev can change these limits or approve an exception. The two arms must share portfolio-level exposure and correlation accounting, not merely independent per-arm caps.
14. **Risk-first sizing.** Fractional Kelly is a target sizing method only when the needed probabilities/payoff estimates are validated and the risk policy enables it. Until then use conservative, explicitly configured risk sizing. An uncalibrated model confidence is not a Kelly input.
15. **Execution confirms reality.** The execution engine routes authorized intents, manages orders, controls slippage, and confirms fills. Broker acknowledgements, rejects, partial fills, cancellations, positions, stops, exits, and reconciliation drive operational state. Simulated fills stay in simulation.
16. **Session and emergency safety.** Current policy requires pre-close flattening for the applicable trading session and no overnight positions. Monitoring earnings outside market hours does not authorize overnight exposure. Nick explicitly confirmed on 13 October 2026 that he does not want to hold overnight. This resolves PR #29's earlier multi-day proposal in favor of same-session exits for both arms. Cancel pending entries and start closing owned exposure in the configured pre-close buffer using the broker's actual regular-session calendar, including early closes. Recover old positions immediately when trading is available. A halted market, outage or failed execution can prevent flatness; cancellation or acknowledgement alone is not a fill, and an unresolved exit requires visible operator attention. Halt blocks new entries first; unresolved exposure remains visible until broker-confirmed flat. This canon does not enable LIVE authority.
17. **Exit and review.** Each arm supports planned/adaptive exits, P&L attribution, and a Decision Episode connecting the original state, context, proposal, risk verdict, execution, management transitions, and realized outcome.
18. **Reality/outcome is the evidence.** Capture P&L, MFE/MAE, fill quality/slippage, prediction accuracy, time in trade, and opportunity cost. Store observed and simulated outcomes distinctly. No LLM response is proof of an executed trade or a successful result.
19. **Replay engine.** Replay episodes with a temporal firewall and controlled counterfactual simulation. Freeze data, policies, model identities, and evaluation inputs so changes can be attributed.
20. **Wisdom extraction.** Identify candidate lessons through outcome attribution and pattern discovery. Preserve rejected hypotheses and failure modes as negative knowledge. Candidate lessons remain separate from validated knowledge.
21. **Validation lab.** Evaluate candidate changes using suitable walk-forward, out-of-sample, and statistical checks against frozen baselines and meaningful control treatments. Bound the adaptive surface. Sample insufficiency means insufficient evidence.
22. **Versioned knowledge store.** Promote only supported lessons/rules/models, with source episodes, evaluation receipts, versions, and rollback history. Later decisions retrieve the promoted version; promotion cannot rewrite historical evidence.
23. **Continuous wisdom loop.** Observe market → generate thesis/prediction → execute an authorized trade → manage with APDE → exit → replay → extract wisdom → validate/promote → inform later decisions. Overnight replay is part of the target operating rhythm; a completed trade alone does not establish learning.
24. **Performance principles and intended outcomes.** Probability over prediction, expectancy over win rate, risk first, continuous adaptation, and disciplined use of data. Seek stronger risk management, better decisions, consistent process, and accumulated validated knowledge. Returns and improvement are objectives to measure, not promises.
25. **Core ownership.** Athena Core remains domain-neutral and owns general episode/context/lesson/validation concepts. Athena Trading owns both strategies, market/broker semantics, APDE policy, trading risk, and trading outcomes. Integrate through athena_adapter; do not copy another independent Core into this repo.
26. **Keep one product workflow.** Share contracts, state, risk, execution, replay integration, and reporting across both arms. Provider choice, research ablations, and deployment vendors are implementation choices, not additional product architectures.
27. **Two model roles, one deterministic authority boundary.** Astra 6 proposes a schema-validated plan; Jev judges a code-defined action set against the same underlying evidence and proposal. Entry may use TAKE_TRADE, NO_TRADE and INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE; management choices are bounded by state and policy. A Jev acceptance is still only a proposal to deterministic risk. Record both exact model identities, prompt/question and schema versions, evidence/input hashes, raw/validated outputs, expiry, invalidation, decision reason and risk verdict. Agreement is not independent proof; correlated mistakes and anchoring remain evaluation risks. Neither model's confidence is a measured probability of trading profit, a forecast probability or a Kelly input. Do not manufacture repeated agreement through retries or secretly substitute a model. The existing pilot's Jev-only thresholds remain documented current behavior in [LIVE_PAPER.md](LIVE_PAPER.md), not an approved hybrid acceptance policy. That pilot may submit, once per session and only under PAPER, the highest-scoring TAKE_TRADE label already seen when no order has been sent by the configured cutoff, and only when the fresh completed bar is still that same setup. A stale bar or a changed setup does not send the old order.
28. **Active paper is the immediate operating target.** Nick explicitly requested current market data and actual broker paper orders, with Jev in the existing active entry path; a shadow stage is not required. The next build target adds Astra 6 and bounded hybrid entry/management through the same runtime, after its acceptance gates pass. Missing, malformed, uncertain, contradictory, or unavailable adjudication cannot approve new exposure. Critical protection and emergency/session exits do not depend on Astra 6 or Jev being available. Real-money LIVE authority remains disabled. Start with the bounded intraday paper slice described in implementation status, then extend through the same workflow.

29. **Cloud operation is the deployment target.** Nick requested an always-on cloud runtime independent of his local computer. Persist decision and broker state, restart after interruption, follow broker market sessions, and reconcile unfinished exposure before new entries. Being online continuously does not authorize trading outside the configured session, overnight exposure, real-money execution, or unvalidated learning. The deployment runbook describes the current host and Jev API. Astra 6 is the owner-selected LLM; its exact provider deployment/API model ID, account access and SDK support are not verified. Resolve and pin them explicitly before integration, without guessing or silently choosing an alternative. That runbook's host examples are a Hostinger VPS and a DigitalOcean Droplet.

30. **Evaluate every completed trade.** Reconcile actual broker fills, record attributable outcomes and preserve failed/no-fill episodes. Make verified observed experience available to subsequent eligible decisions, with account/strategy identity, provenance and availability timestamps. Unmeasured quantities remain unknown.
31. **Review the day after after-hours.** The cloud supervisor runs the daily review at or after 20:15 America/New_York and catches up after downtime. Record candidates, rejected hypotheses, prospective validation results, promotions and unresolved data. Defer promotion while exposure or outcome integrity is unresolved.
32. **Improve through measured, bounded changes.** Intraday experience retrieval is permitted; policy changes need separate prospective evidence. Initial automatic promotion can only add a temporary NO_TRADE rule, never increase exposure or change model weights/code. Preserve fixed study criteria, expiry and rejection history. Every trade supplies information; no trade guarantees an improvement.

33. **Facts and hypotheses stay separate.** Source facts retain links/record IDs, publication/event/availability times, provenance, corrections and freshness. Calculated features and validated forecasts retain method/version identity. Astra 6's interpretation, scenario assumptions and proposed actions are labeled model hypotheses. Treat fetched content as untrusted data, never instructions; schema/provenance validation and deterministic enforcement are required.
34. **Event-driven, bounded updates.** Reassess on material new source evidence, relevant price/regime changes, thesis expiry/invalidation or permitted APDE events. Deduplicate inputs, enforce per-episode/session call, cost, latency and retry limits, and recheck freshness after model work immediately before any new exposure. Every plan has a horizon, expiry and invalidation. No unrestricted reasoning loop or model call on every tick is required.
35. **Fail closed for new exposure, keep protection alive.** Missing, stale, malformed, mismatched, contradictory, timed-out or unavailable model results cannot authorize entry or ADD. Never reuse an expired plan or treat model silence as approval. Existing protection and mandatory risk-reducing actions remain in the deterministic control path while model work is pending or failed. A broker outage can still prevent confirmed execution; unresolved exposure remains visible.
36. **Aggressive within policy.** Seek valid opportunity capture, timely reassessment and adaptive winner management. This does not raise risk/exposure/call limits, loosen stops, add leverage, authorize overnight holds or enable LIVE. Any later policy expansion is a separate explicit decision with evidence.
37. **Acceptance requires full diagram alignment.** The [hybrid contract](HYBRID_MODEL_CONTRACT.md) maps every major diagram block to deliverables and evidence. Neither two model adapters nor a second paper strategy completes the image. Full APDE, validated forecasts, shared portfolio risk, Core replay/validation/knowledge integration and operator recovery each require demonstrated behavior.

## Shared decision flow

| Stage | Responsibility | Output |
|---|---|---|
| World state | Assemble only available evidence and reconciled account state | Frozen state reference |
| Athena context | Retrieve relevant validated knowledge | Versioned context reference |
| Strategy / calculation / forecast code | Construct supported candidates, calculated features and validated forecast outputs | Bounded actions and labeled quantitative evidence |
| Astra 6 generative LLM | Interpret source evidence and context; propose thesis, scenarios and plan | Structured hypotheses/proposal with expiry and invalidation |
| Jev bounded judge | Assess underlying evidence plus proposal; choose a permitted action or abstain | Recorded typed judgment; no execution authority |
| Deterministic risk | Authorize, constrain, or refuse under policy | Exact authorized intent or refusal |
| Execution + APDE | Route and manage, then reconcile broker events | Observed execution/management events |
| Outcome + Core learning | Attribute, replay, extract, validate, and promote | Decision Episode and versioned knowledge |

Strategy and validated computation supply the candidate/evidence boundary; Astra 6 proposes, Jev judges, and deterministic code authorizes and enforces. Both strategy arms share this design. Critical protection bypasses model latency and failure. The original visual is preserved; the 13 October hybrid amendment supersedes the earlier Jev-only amendment without rewriting historical experiments.
